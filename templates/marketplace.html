Why Lumos Marketplace

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the seller doesn't deposit USDC?

Each phase of a trade has a 20-minute timeout. If the seller fails to deposit USDC within that window after you join, the trade expires automatically and you owe nothing — no KRW has been sent, and no USDC has been locked.

What if I send the KRW but the seller never releases USDC?

After confirming KRW payment on-chain, you have a 24-hour self-claim window. If the seller doesn't release within that period, you can claim the locked USDC directly from the escrow contract — no support ticket required.

Do I need to register or sign up?

No accounts, no email, no KYC at the platform level. Your MetaMask wallet is your identity. Connect, join a trade, and you're done.

Why is the marketplace empty right now?

Listings appear here only while a seller is actively waiting for a buyer. The moment a buyer joins a listing, it disappears from the marketplace — preventing two people from trying to claim the same USDC. If you don't see anything listed, it just means no seller is currently waiting.

Which network does Lumos use?

Polygon mainnet only. Make sure your MetaMask is on Polygon and you have a small amount of POL for gas (typically less than ₩100 per transaction).
